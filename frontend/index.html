The Iron Shears Story

Founded in 2014 by master barber Marcus Bell, The Iron Shears was born from a passion for traditional barbering techniques and a desire to bring them back to the modern man. We believe a great haircut is more than just a style – it's a ritual, a moment of self-care, and an opportunity to look and feel your best.

Every appointment is a chance to connect, to craft something tailored to you, and to create an experience worth savoring. We use only premium products and time-honored techniques, because your grooming deserves nothing less.
