The Iron Shears Journey

Founded with a passion for traditional barbering, we blend heritage techniques with modern style.

Craftsmanship You Can Trust

At The Iron Shears, every cut begins with a conversation. Our master barbers take the time to understand your lifestyle, hair type, and desired look before picking up a pair of shears. We believe the barber chair is a confessional — a place where stories are shared and confidence is shaped.

Founded in 2014 by Marcus Bell, our shop was built on the principle that a great barber doesn't just cut hair — they craft an experience. From the warm towel to the precise line-up, every step is performed with meticulous attention to detail.

We source only the finest products: premium scissors hand-forged in Japan, natural barbering oils, and shampoos free of harsh chemicals. Our commitment to quality extends to every tool that touches your hair.
